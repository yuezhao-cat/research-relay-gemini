Active sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts rarely remain bare during the oxygen reduction reaction (ORR), so the surface coverage under working conditions determines how the sites bind the ORR intermediates. Here, we prepare an FeCoMn–NC catalyst in which bcc FeCo alloy particles coexist with Mn single atoms. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC reaches a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE, higher than those of commercial Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC, and retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy shows that the species at the catalyst surface change with potential, so we calculated the surface coverage under working conditions. At 0.78 V, the FeCo particles are covered by O*, and the adjacent Mn single atoms carry an axial OH. The axial OH pulls Mn out of the N4 plane toward itself, and an ORR intermediate binding to Mn from the opposite side draws Mn back into the plane. Pulling Mn back consumes part of the binding energy and weakens the binding of the ORR intermediates. As a result, OH* removal is downhill at 0.78 V, while OOH* formation is still nearly thermoneutral, placing FeCoMn–NC near the apex of the activity volcano.

直译：热解金属–氮–碳（M–N–C）催化剂中的活性位点在氧还原反应（ORR）中很少保持裸露，因此工作条件下的表面覆盖决定了位点如何结合 ORR 中间体。本文制备了一种体心立方 FeCo 合金颗粒与 Mn 单原子共存的 FeCoMn–NC 催化剂。在 0.1 M KOH 中，FeCoMn–NC 的半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于 RHE），高于商业 Pt/C、FeCo–NC 和 Mn–NC，并在 238 h 后保持初始电流的 96.5%。原位红外光谱表明催化剂表面的物种随电位变化，因此我们计算了工作条件下的表面覆盖。在 0.78 V 下，FeCo 颗粒被 O* 覆盖，相邻的 Mn 单原子带轴向 OH。轴向 OH 把 Mn 拉出 N4 平面、偏向自己一侧，而从对侧与 Mn 结合的 ORR 中间体又把 Mn 拉回平面。拉回 Mn 消耗了部分结合能，削弱了 ORR 中间体的结合。因此，OH* 脱附在 0.78 V 下为下坡，而 OOH* 生成仍接近热中性，使 FeCoMn–NC 位于活性火山图顶点附近。
