## 综合版英文摘要

Metal particles commonly coexist with single-atom sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts, yet catalytic models often rely on bare single-atom surfaces. How the surface state formed under working conditions governs oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) intermediate binding remains unclear. Here, we prepare an FeCoMn–NC catalyst combining bcc FeCo alloy particles with Mn single atoms. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC delivers a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE, surpassing commercial Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC, and retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy reveals potential-dependent changes in interfacial species, motivating surface-coverage calculations under ORR conditions. At 0.78 V versus RHE, these calculations favor O*-covered FeCo particles and Mn single atoms coordinated by an axial OH. Without the axial OH, Mn binds ORR intermediates too strongly. The axial OH pulls Mn out of the N₄ plane, and an ORR intermediate binding on the opposite side pulls Mn back into the plane. This opposing displacement offsets part of the adsorption energy and moderates the overly strong binding, which explains the highest ORR activity among the catalysts studied here. Consequently, OH* removal becomes downhill at 0.78 V while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral, placing FeCoMn–NC near the apex of the calculated ORR activity volcano.

## 中文直译

热解金属–氮–碳（M–N–C）催化剂中金属颗粒通常与单原子位点共存，但催化反应模型往往依赖裸露的单原子表面。工作条件下形成的表面状态如何主导氧还原反应（ORR）中间体的结合仍不清楚。本文制备了一种 bcc FeCo 合金颗粒与 Mn 单原子共存的 FeCoMn–NC 催化剂。在 0.1 M KOH 中，FeCoMn–NC 的半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于 RHE），优于商业 Pt/C、FeCo–NC 和 Mn–NC，并在 238 h 后保持 96.5% 的初始电流。原位红外光谱揭示了界面物种随电位变化的特征，据此开展了 ORR 条件下的表面覆盖度计算。计算结果表明，在 0.78 V（相对于 RHE）下，FeCo 颗粒表面覆盖 O*，相邻 Mn 单原子带有轴向 OH。没有轴向 OH 时，Mn 对 ORR 中间体结合过强。轴向 OH 将 Mn 拉出 N₄ 平面，从对侧吸附的 ORR 中间体又将 Mn 拉回平面内。这种对向位移抵消了部分吸附能、调节了过强结合，从而解释了所研究催化剂中最高的 ORR 活性。因此，OH* 脱附在 0.78 V 下变为下坡，OOH* 生成仍接近热中性，使 FeCoMn–NC 位于计算所得 ORR 活性火山图顶点附近。
