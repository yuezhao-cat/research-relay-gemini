# GPT mechanism revision for preferred abstract (2026-11-20)

## Point of disagreement

Preferred abstract chain "weak axial Mn–OH → dz² close to EF → OOH* forms → downhill ORR → volcano apex" is **not yet a demonstrated mechanism**. It misuses PDOS proximity as a proven causal link and neglects the strongest counterfactual evidence from T032/T041/T042. The manuscript should explain why the **same FeCoMn–NC Mn–OH site** has favorable adsorption energetics, not simply why it ranks between two other catalysts.

## Proposed replacement mechanism paragraph (English)

The axial OH displaces Mn from the N₄ coordination plane, while adsorption of ORR intermediates on the opposite side drives Mn back toward the plane. This structural reorganization introduces an energetic penalty without necessarily weakening local Mn–adsorbate interactions, thereby alleviating the excessively strong intermediate binding of the unligated Mn site. Consequently, OH* removal becomes more favorable while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral at 0.78 V, yielding a balanced ORR free-energy profile.

## 中文

轴向 OH 使 Mn 原子偏离 N₄ 配位平面，而 ORR 中间体从另一侧吸附时又促使 Mn 返回平面附近。这种结构重组增加了吸附的能量代价，却不一定削弱 Mn 与中间体之间的局部成键，从而缓解了没有轴向配体时 Mn 位点对中间体的过强吸附。因此，OH* 脱除变得更加有利，同时 OOH* 在 0.78 V 下的形成仍接近热中性，使整个 ORR 反应具有更加均衡的自由能分布。

## Evidence and limits

- T032: same FeCo/8O* background, unligated Mn U_L 0.516 V vs Mn–OH 0.776 V; need express as model/site comparison.
- T042: unadsorbed axOH Mn offset ~0.442 Å; OOH* and OH* adsorption draws Mn nearly into plane; do not generalize exactly to O*.
- T041: extra substrate distortion OH* +0.236 eV and OOH* +0.432 eV; frozen interaction OH* +0.100 eV and OOH* −0.100 eV. Thus avoid unconditional statement electronic interactions strengthen for every intermediate. This decomposition is thermodynamic static energy, not activation barrier.
- PDOS/spin-polarized dz² may be **supporting electronic characterization**, but no demonstrated causal chain merely from closeness to Fermi level.
- T039 strong particle-induced Mn–OH overoxidation suppression claim remains electronically confounded; don't close abstract by reasserting particle causality.
