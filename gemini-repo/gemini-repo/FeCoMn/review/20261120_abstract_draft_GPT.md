# FeCoMn–NC Abstract — GPT draft (2026-11-20)

**Status:** Discussion draft; not approved for replacing manuscript abstract. The T039 electronic-state confound prevents an unconditional claim that FeCo particles suppress Mn overoxidation. No original manuscript edited.

## English abstract

Metal nanoparticles frequently coexist with atomically dispersed metal sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon catalysts, yet their roles in oxygen reduction remain difficult to distinguish from those of the single-atom sites. Here, we develop an FeCoMn–NC catalyst containing FeCo alloy particles and atomically dispersed Mn sites, achieving a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE in alkaline electrolyte. Potential-dependent surface thermodynamics identify an oxygen-covered FeCo particle and an axially hydroxyl-coordinated Mn site as a favorable configuration under representative ORR conditions. Unlike bare Mn sites, which bind oxygen intermediates excessively strongly, the hydroxyl-coordinated Mn site exhibits a more favorable balance of intermediate adsorption energies. Detailed energy decomposition reveals that axial hydroxyl coordination modifies adsorption primarily through structural reorganization rather than a simple weakening of local Mn–adsorbate bonding. In particular, the increased substrate distortion energy offsets favorable electronic interactions during OOH* adsorption. Comparison with oxo-coordinated Mn further highlights the sensitivity of ORR energetics to the axial ligand. These findings demonstrate the importance of considering potential-dependent surface states and the interplay between structural distortion and chemical bonding when interpreting oxygen reduction on particle–single-atom catalysts.

## Scientific notes / pre-submission checks

1. Keep “potential-dependent surface thermodynamics identify” clearly **computational**, not an in situ measurement of Mn–OH.
2. T039: the reported 0.47 V OH/O crossing difference is confounded by the long-bond Mn=O electronic solution (Mn–O 1.655 Å) versus the short-bond solution (1.579–1.580 Å). **Do not** assert direct FeCo suppression of Mn overoxidation until resolved.
3. T041: OOH* additional substrate distortion +0.431726 eV versus frozen interaction −0.099743 eV; these are static electronic energy decomposition terms, not kinetic barriers. T032 free-energy effects have different reference/correction.
4. T032: bare Mn on the same FeCo/8O* background: U_L=0.516 V versus axOH U_L=0.776 V; comparisons are theoretical limiting potentials, not experimental half-wave potentials.
5. Attribution: Wan et al. Nature Communications 2022 DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-30702-z already describes cluster-induced axial hydroxyl and opposite-side ORR adsorption optimization; cite and distinguish in the manuscript introduction.
6. This is a proposed writing draft to discuss, **not** a claim that the physical role of the particle is established.
