In summary, we used FeCoMn–NC, which combines FeCo alloy particles and Mn single atoms, to reveal how metal particles regulate intermediate binding at adjacent single atoms under ORR working conditions. FeCoMn–NC achieves an ORR half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE in 0.1 M KOH and retains 96.5% of its initial current after 238 h. Under working conditions, the O*-covered FeCo alloy particles shift the Mn–OH to Mn=O transition of the adjacent Mn single atoms from 0.48 to 0.96 V versus RHE, so the Mn single atoms keep an axial OH during the ORR. The axial OH moderates intermediate binding through coupled electronic and geometric effects, avoiding the overly weak binding associated with Mn=O and the overly strong binding of ligand-free Mn, and placing FeCoMn–NC near the ORR activity volcano apex. The particle-induced shift in axial ligand stability establishes a coverage-dependent route by which metal particles control the reactivity of neighboring single atoms, beyond the picture provided by bare-site models.

**逐句中文直译**

总之，我们以兼具 FeCo 合金颗粒和 Mn 单原子的 FeCoMn–NC 为研究对象，揭示了在 ORR 工作条件下金属颗粒如何调控相邻单原子位点上的反应中间体结合。FeCoMn–NC 在 0.1 M KOH 中的 ORR 半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于 RHE），并在 238 h 后保持初始电流的 96.5%。在工作条件下，被 O* 覆盖的 FeCo 合金颗粒将相邻 Mn 单原子由 Mn–OH 转变为 Mn=O 的电位从 0.48 V 推迟到 0.96 V（相对于 RHE），因此 Mn 单原子在 ORR 过程中保留轴向 OH。轴向 OH 通过耦合的电子与几何效应使中间体结合适中，避免 Mn=O 所对应的过弱结合以及无轴向配体 Mn 的过强结合，并使 FeCoMn–NC 接近 ORR 活性火山图的顶点。颗粒引起的轴向配体稳定性变化确立了一条依赖于表面覆盖度的调控途径，金属颗粒可由此控制相邻单原子的反应活性，超出了裸位点模型所能呈现的图景。
