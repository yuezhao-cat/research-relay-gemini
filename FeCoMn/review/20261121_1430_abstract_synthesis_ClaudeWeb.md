## 英文
Metal particles commonly coexist with single-atom sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts, yet catalytic models often rely on bare single-atom surfaces. How the surface state formed under working conditions governs the binding of oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) intermediates therefore remains unclear. Here, we prepare an FeCoMn–NC catalyst in which FeCo alloy particles coexist with Mn single atoms. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC reaches a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus the reversible hydrogen electrode (RHE), higher than those of commercial Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC, and retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy shows that the interfacial species change with potential, so we calculated the surface coverage under working conditions. At 0.78 V versus RHE, the FeCo particles are covered by O*, and the adjacent Mn single atoms carry an axial OH. The axial OH pulls Mn out of the N4 plane toward itself, and an ORR intermediate binding to Mn from the opposite side pulls Mn back to the plane. This displacement offsets part of the binding energy and moderates the over-strong binding of the ORR intermediates on Mn. As a result, OH* removal is downhill at 0.78 V, while OOH* formation is still nearly thermoneutral. FeCoMn–NC therefore lies near the apex of the activity volcano, which explains its highest measured activity.

## 中文直译
热解金属–氮–碳（M–N–C）催化剂中金属颗粒通常与单原子位点共存，但催化模型往往依赖裸露的单原子表面。因此，工作条件下形成的表面状态如何支配氧还原反应（ORR）中间体的结合仍不清楚。本文制备了一种 FeCo 合金颗粒与 Mn 单原子共存的 FeCoMn–NC 催化剂。在 0.1 M KOH 中，FeCoMn–NC 的半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于可逆氢电极 RHE），高于商业 Pt/C、FeCo–NC 和 Mn–NC，并在 238 h 后保持初始电流的 96.5%。原位红外光谱表明界面物种随电位变化，因此我们计算了工作条件下的表面覆盖。在 0.78 V（相对于 RHE）下，FeCo 颗粒被 O* 覆盖，相邻的 Mn 单原子带轴向 OH。轴向 OH 把 Mn 拉出 N4 平面、偏向自己一侧，而从对侧与 Mn 结合的 ORR 中间体又把 Mn 拉回平面。这一位移抵消了部分结合能，缓和了 Mn 对 ORR 中间体的过强结合。因此，OH* 脱附在 0.78 V 下为下坡，而 OOH* 生成仍接近热中性。FeCoMn–NC 因而位于活性火山图顶点附近，这解释了它实测活性最高的原因。
