## 推荐拼合版（英文，四句）

In summary, we prepared an FeCoMn–NC catalyst combining FeCo alloy particles and Mn single atoms, achieving an ORR half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE in 0.1 M KOH and retaining 96.5% of its initial current after 238 h. The O*-covered FeCo alloy particles shift the Mn–OH to Mn=O transition of the adjacent Mn single atoms from 0.48 to 0.96 V versus RHE, preserving an axial OH during the ORR. The axial OH balances intermediate binding between the overly strong adsorption on ligand-free Mn and the overly weak adsorption on Mn=O, placing FeCoMn–NC near the apex of the ORR activity volcano. The results establish a specific role for O*-covered FeCo particles: by shifting the potential at which adjacent Mn atoms change their axial ligand, the particles regulate intermediate binding in a way that bare single-atom models cannot capture.
