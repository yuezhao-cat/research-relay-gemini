## Suggested title

*Operating-State Axial Hydroxyl Coordination Regulates Oxygen Reduction on Mn Sites in FeCoMn–NC*

## English Abstract

Metal particles commonly coexist with single-atom sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts, yet catalytic models often rely on bare single-atom surfaces. How the surface states formed under working conditions influence oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) intermediate binding remains unclear. Here, we prepare an FeCoMn–NC catalyst combining FeCo alloy particles with atomically dispersed Mn–N₄ sites. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC achieves a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE, outperforming commercial Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC, and retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy reveals potential-dependent changes in interfacial species, motivating surface-coverage calculations under ORR conditions. At 0.78 V versus RHE, these calculations favor O*-covered FeCo particles and Mn–N₄ sites coordinated by an axial OH. Without this axial ligand, Mn binds ORR intermediates too strongly, making OH* removal unfavorable. Axial OH coordination displaces Mn from the N₄ plane, whereas adsorption of OOH* or OH* on the opposite side draws Mn back toward the plane. The associated structural deformation weakens the overall adsorption without necessarily weakening local Mn–adsorbate bonding. Consequently, OH* removal becomes downhill while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral at 0.78 V, placing FeCoMn–NC near the apex of the calculated ORR activity volcano.

## 中文直译

金属颗粒通常与单原子位点共存于热解制备的金属–氮–碳（M–N–C）催化剂中，但催化反应模型往往仍以裸露的单原子表面为基础。工作条件下形成的表面状态如何影响氧还原反应（ORR）中间体的结合仍有待阐明。本文制备了一种 FeCo 合金颗粒与原子级分散 Mn–N₄ 位点共存的 FeCoMn–NC 催化剂。在 0.1 M KOH 中，FeCoMn–NC 的半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于可逆氢电极），优于商业 Pt/C、FeCo–NC 和 Mn–NC，并在连续运行 238 h 后保持初始电流的 96.5%。原位红外光谱揭示了界面物种随电位变化的特征，因此进一步通过表面覆盖度计算分析了 ORR 工作条件下的表面状态。计算表明，在 0.78 V（相对于可逆氢电极）下，FeCo 颗粒表面倾向于被 O* 覆盖，而 Mn–N₄ 位点倾向于形成轴向 OH 配位。没有该轴向配体时，Mn 对 ORR 中间体的结合过强，使 OH* 脱除在热力学上不利。轴向 OH 配位使 Mn 偏离 N₄ 平面，而 OOH* 或 OH* 从相反一侧吸附时又促使 Mn 返回平面附近。由此产生的结构形变削弱了整体吸附强度，却不一定削弱局部 Mn 与中间体之间的化学成键。因此，在 0.78 V 下，OH* 脱除转为热力学下坡，而 OOH* 形成仍接近热中性，使 FeCoMn–NC 位于计算得到的 ORR 活性火山图顶点附近。

## Editorial decisions and scientific basis

1. Uses the structure of the earlier proposal 2: metal-particle/single-atom coexistence -> experimental activity -> potential-dependent operando interface -> computed covered states -> matched no-axOH control -> geometry and adsorption -> theoretical ORR energetic balance.
2. Does not claim that FeCo particles *determine* the axial ligand or suppress Mn overoxidation by 0.47 V because T039 suffers electronic-state incomparability of the original long-bond Mn=O structure vs the short-bond solution.
3. T032: matched FeCo/8O* model has U_L=0.516 V without axial OH vs 0.776 V with axial OH. At U=0.78 V OH* removal is unfavorable in noOH and favorable in axOH.
4. T042: axOH Mn originally out of N₄ plane ~0.442 Å; OOH* and OH* adsorption draw it toward the plane. This statement does NOT generalize to O*.
5. T041 energy decomposition: for OH* added substrate distortion ~+0.236 eV and frozen electronic interaction ~+0.100 eV; for OOH* added distortion ~+0.432 eV, frozen interaction ~−0.100 eV, fragment distortion ~−0.065 eV. The statement "without necessarily weakening local bonding" is deliberate; do not imply bonding strengthened for every adsorbate. Static energy components are NOT kinetic barriers.
6. Operando IR is described only as showing changes in interfacial species; no claim of direct observation of Mn–OH or unique verification of OOH* with a potentially overlapping band.
7. The theoretical volcano is not claimed to quantitatively prove experimental E1/2 differences. The title aligns to the supported axial-ligand mechanism; a sharper FeCo-specific causal title would require new decisive evidence.
