## 三、融合定稿候选全文（199 词，方案二底稿 + 方案一亮点）

### English Abstract

Metal particles commonly coexist with single-atom sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts, yet catalytic models often rely on bare single-atom surfaces. How the surface state formed under working conditions governs oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) intermediate binding remains unclear. Here, we prepare an FeCoMn–NC catalyst combining FeCo alloy particles with dispersed Mn–N₄ sites. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC delivers a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus the reversible hydrogen electrode (RHE), surpassing commercial Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC, and retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy detects potential-dependent interfacial water changes and OOH* formation, indicating an associative pathway on a covered surface. Surface-coverage calculations at 0.78 V versus RHE reveal O*-covered FeCo particles alongside Mn–N₄ sites carrying an axial OH. Without this axial OH, Mn binds ORR intermediates too strongly and impedes OH* removal. The axial OH pulls Mn out of the N₄ plane, and an ORR intermediate adsorbing on the opposite side draws Mn back toward the plane. This opposing displacement offsets part of the adsorption energy and moderates the overly strong binding. Consequently, OH* removal becomes downhill at 0.78 V while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral, placing FeCoMn–NC near the ORR activity volcano apex.

### 中文直译

金属颗粒通常与单原子位点共存于热解金属氮碳（M–N–C）催化剂中，但催化模型往往仍依赖裸露的单原子表面。工作条件下形成的表面状态如何支配氧还原反应（ORR）中间体结合仍不明确。本文中，我们制备了一种结合 FeCo 合金颗粒与分散 Mn–N₄ 位点的 FeCoMn–NC 催化剂。在 0.1 M KOH 中，FeCoMn–NC 的半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于可逆氢电极 RHE），超过商业 Pt/C、FeCo–NC 和 Mn–NC，且在 238 h 内保持 96.5% 的初始电流。原位红外光谱检测到随电位变化的界面水信号与 OOH* 生成，表明反应在被覆盖的表面上沿缔合路径进行。在 0.78 V（相对于 RHE）下的表面覆盖度计算揭示了 O* 覆盖的 FeCo 颗粒以及带有轴向 OH 的 Mn–N₄ 位点。没有该轴向 OH 时，Mn 对 ORR 中间体结合过强并阻碍 OH* 脱附。轴向 OH 将 Mn 拉出 N₄ 平面，而从对侧吸附的 ORR 中间体又将 Mn 拉回平面附近。这种反向位移抵消了部分吸附能，从而缓和了过强的结合。因此，在 0.78 V 下 OH* 脱附转为下坡，同时 OOH* 生成保持接近热中性，使 FeCoMn–NC 位于 ORR 活性火山图顶点附近。
