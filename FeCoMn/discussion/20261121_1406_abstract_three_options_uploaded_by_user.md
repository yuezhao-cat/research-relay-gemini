## 一、核心难点诊断与破题思路

本篇摘要的核心张力在于，实验设计天然围绕“FeCo 颗粒与 Mn 单原子共存”展开，但受限于双电子态分支的边界，全文已不能宣称颗粒决定了轴向配体或推迟了 Mn 过氧化，而新确立的机理落脚点是工作态下 `Mn–N₄` 的轴向 `OH` 通过几何拉回缓解过强吸附。破局的关键在于**重构开篇的科学问题**，将其从“颗粒如何决定单原子配体”精准收束为“复合催化剂在真实工作条件下呈现何种表面覆盖态，以及该工作态如何调和中间体结合强弱”。在机理表述上，弃用抽象的“结构重组”与“能量代价”，改用“裸位点吸附过强、轴向 `OH` 将 Mn 拉出 `N₄` 平面、对侧吸附将 Mn 拉回平面并消耗部分结合能”的具象物理链条，既无需借助防御性对照句，也能严密闭环地解释为何 `0.78 V` 下 `OH*` 脱附转为下坡而 `OOH*` 生成仍接近热中性。

## 二、三套 *ACS Catalysis* 投稿级摘要方案

### 方案一：稳健聚焦工作态表面配位（首尾彻底对齐，推荐作为投稿基准）

**English Abstract (204 words)**

Pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts are often modeled as bare single-atom sites, yet their active surfaces evolve with potential. Clarifying how surface species formed under working conditions regulate intermediate binding is essential for understanding oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) activity. Here, we synthesize a FeCoMn–NC catalyst containing bcc FeCo alloy particles and atomically dispersed Mn–N₄ sites. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC reaches a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE, outperforming Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC. It also retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy detects potential-dependent interfacial water changes and OOH* formation, indicating an associative pathway on a covered surface. Surface-coverage calculations show that at 0.78 V versus RHE, FeCo particles are covered by O* while adjacent Mn–N₄ sites carry an axial OH. Without this axial ligand, Mn binds ORR intermediates too strongly and impedes OH* removal. The axial OH pulls Mn out of the N₄ plane, and an ORR intermediate binding on the opposite side draws Mn back toward the plane. Pulling Mn back consumes part of the binding energy and weakens the excessive adsorption. At 0.78 V, this moderation turns OH* removal downhill while keeping OOH* formation nearly thermoneutral, placing FeCoMn–NC near the activity volcano apex.

**中文直译**

热解金属氮碳（M–N–C）催化剂通常被建模为裸露的单原子位点，但它们的活性表面会随电位演变。阐明工作条件下形成的表面物种如何调控中间体结合，对于理解氧还原反应（ORR）活性至关重要。本文中，我们合成了一种包含体心立方 FeCo 合金颗粒与原子级分散 Mn–N₄ 位点的 FeCoMn–NC 催化剂。在 0.1 M KOH 中，FeCoMn–NC 的半波电位达到 0.885 V（相对于可逆氢电极），优于 Pt/C、FeCo–NC 和 Mn–NC。它在连续运行 238 h 后仍保持 96.5% 的初始电流。原位红外光谱检测到随电位变化的界面水信号与 OOH* 生成，表明反应在被物种覆盖的表面上沿缔合路径进行。表面覆盖度计算表明，在 0.78 V（相对于可逆氢电极）下，FeCo 颗粒表面覆盖着 O*，而相邻的 Mn–N₄ 位点带有轴向 OH。没有该轴向配体时，Mn 对 ORR 中间体结合过强并阻碍 OH* 脱附。轴向 OH 将 Mn 拉出 N₄ 平面，而从对侧结合的 ORR 中间体又将 Mn 拉回平面附近。将 Mn 拉回的过程消耗了部分结合能，从而削弱了过强的吸附。在 0.78 V 下，这种调控使 OH* 脱附转为下坡，同时使 OOH* 生成保持接近热中性，使 FeCoMn–NC 位于活性火山图顶点附近。

### 方案二：保留颗粒与单原子共存背景（自然衔接，不越界宣称颗粒因果）

**English Abstract (203 words)**

Metal particles commonly coexist with single-atom sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts, yet catalytic models often rely on bare single-atom surfaces. How the surface state formed under working conditions governs oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) intermediate binding remains unclear. Here, we prepare a FeCoMn–NC catalyst combining bcc FeCo alloy particles with dispersed Mn–N₄ sites. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC delivers a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE, surpassing commercial Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC. It further retains 96.5% of its initial current over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy tracks potential-dependent interfacial water evolution and OOH* accumulation, showing that ORR proceeds via an associative pathway on a covered surface. Surface-coverage calculations at 0.78 V versus RHE reveal O*-covered FeCo particles alongside Mn–N₄ sites coordinated by an axial OH. Whereas bare Mn–N₄ sites bind ORR intermediates too strongly, the axial OH pulls Mn out of the N₄ plane. When an ORR intermediate adsorbs on the opposite side, it draws Mn back toward the N₄ plane. This opposing displacement offsets part of the adsorption energy and moderates the overly strong binding. Consequently, OH* removal becomes downhill at 0.78 V while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral, placing FeCoMn–NC near the ORR activity volcano apex.

### 方案三：精炼高冲击力版（句式紧凑，画面鲜明）

**English Abstract (180 words)**

Active sites in pyrolyzed metal–nitrogen–carbon (M–N–C) catalysts rarely remain bare during the oxygen reduction reaction (ORR), making surface coverage central to catalytic activity. Here, we report a FeCoMn–NC catalyst integrating bcc FeCo alloy particles and atomically dispersed Mn–N₄ sites. In 0.1 M KOH, FeCoMn–NC achieves a half-wave potential of 0.885 V versus RHE, surpassing Pt/C, FeCo–NC, and Mn–NC. It maintains 96.5% current retention over 238 h. Operando infrared spectroscopy captures potential-dependent interfacial water changes and OOH* intermediates, establishing an associative pathway on a covered surface. Coverage calculations at 0.78 V versus RHE show O*-covered FeCo particles and adjacent Mn–N₄ sites carrying an axial OH under reaction conditions. Without this axial ligand, Mn binds ORR intermediates excessively strongly. Coordinating the axial OH pulls Mn out of the N₄ plane, whereas ORR intermediate adsorption on the opposite side draws Mn back into the plane. This pull-back displacement consumes part of the binding energy and tempers the overly strong adsorption. Consequently, OH* removal becomes downhill at 0.78 V while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral, placing FeCoMn–NC near the activity volcano apex.
