原文：…the surface coverage is 8O* + Mn–OH* for FeCoMn–NC, 8O* for FeCo–NC, and 1O* for Mn–NC. The corresponding structures are shown in Figure 5g and Figure S24.

Without the particle, the axial ligand on Mn changes from OH to O at 0.48 V, whereas with the particle this change occurs only at 0.96 V (Table S-A). ## 2. 图 6 段第二段（L349–351 之后）补几何机理
原文：…Mn in FeCoMn–NC therefore holds OOH* more firmly, which makes OOH* formation easier than on Mn–NC, where this step limits the ORR.
修改：…Mn in FeCoMn–NC therefore holds OOH* more firmly, which makes OOH* formation easier than on Mn–NC, where this step limits the ORR. 「The axial OH also prevents Mn from binding the intermediates too strongly. Without the axial OH, Mn moves 0.46 Å out of the N4 plane toward an adsorbed OH*, and OH* binds so strongly that its removal limits the ORR at 0.52 V (Table S7 and Table S-C). The axial OH instead pulls Mn 0.44 Å out of the N4 plane toward itself, and an intermediate binding on the opposite side moves Mn back to the plane (Figure S-B). Moving Mn back costs energy. Relative to Mn without the axial OH, the adsorption energy of OH* weakens by 0.34 eV, of which 0.24 eV comes from this structural change and 0.10 eV from the weaker electronic interaction between Mn and OH* (Table S-C). For OOH*, the structural change costs 0.43 eV, whereas the electronic interaction becomes 0.10 eV stronger. As a result, OH* removal becomes downhill at 0.78 V, while OOH* formation remains nearly thermoneutral.」
