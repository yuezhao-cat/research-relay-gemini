## 第二段：加几何机理（为什么不过强）与图 6i 总结句
在 "…where this step limits the ORR." 后插入：
「The axial OH also keeps Mn from binding the intermediates too strongly. Without the axial OH, Mn moves 0.46 Å out of the N4 plane toward an adsorbed OH*, and OH* binds so strongly that its removal limits the ORR at 0.52 V (Table S11). The axial OH instead pulls Mn 0.44 Å out of the N4 plane toward itself, and an intermediate binding on the opposite side draws Mn back toward the plane (Figure S31). Moving Mn back costs energy. Relative to Mn without the axial OH, the adsorption energy of OH* weakens by 0.34 eV, of which 0.24 eV comes from this structural change and 0.10 eV from the weaker electronic interaction between Mn and OH* (Table S11).」
在 "…with the OH bound axially." 后插入：
「The FeCo particles keep this axial OH on Mn, and the axial OH pulls Mn out of the N4 plane, so that Mn binds the intermediates neither too strongly nor too weakly.」
